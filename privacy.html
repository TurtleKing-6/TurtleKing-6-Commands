TurtleOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

TurtleOS is a personal streaming tool used by TurtleKing_6 to run chat commands, points, raffles and alerts on TurtleKing_6's own Twitch and YouTube streams. It runs only on the streamer's own computer. There is no TurtleOS server and no TurtleOS account.

What TurtleOS accesses from Google / YouTube

  • The name and ID of the YouTube channel that signs in.
  • Details of that channel's live broadcast (title, start time, live chat ID).
  • Messages and events in that broadcast's live chat (chat messages, Super Chats, memberships and gifts), so the bot can answer commands and thank supporters.
  • Permission to post chat messages as the connected bot channel.

How the data is used

Only to run the stream's chat features: replying to commands, keeping viewers' loyalty points and watch time, running raffles and the viewer queue, and showing alerts and an activity feed to the streamer. Viewers' YouTube display names, channel IDs, points and watch time are kept so these features work across streams.

Where the data is kept

Everything stays on the streamer's own computer. Sign-in tokens are stored locally and are only sent to Google to talk to the YouTube API. TurtleOS does not send YouTube data to any other server, does not sell or share it, and does not use it for advertising.

Removing access

The channel owner can disconnect YouTube at any time on the TurtleOS YouTube page, or remove TurtleOS's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. TurtleOS's use of YouTube data also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions or data requests: reach TurtleKing_6 on Twitch or in the community Discord.
